Care gaps and deviations

Missing recordRadiology report never produced

The 03/14 lumbar MRI is referenced in three separate notes p. 212, 418, 503 but the radiology report itself does not appear anywhere in the 1,412 pages received. Request it from the imaging provider before any further review.

DeviationIncidental finding never followed up

The supplied standard of care calls for repeat imaging of an incidental renal lesion of this size within 6 to 12 months. No follow-up imaging appears in the 20 months of records that follow p. 503, 587.

Both flags come from the same run. One says a document is missing, the other says the care itself drifted from the standard you supplied, and each points at the page that proves it. This is the whole of phase 1, and it is the only part you need to evaluate before deciding anything else.
