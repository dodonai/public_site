The recommended build · phased

What we'd build, in order

A phased build, sequenced by what the team prioritized on the 15th and by what is lowest-risk to prove first. Each phase maps to the pricing below. Judy's firm-wide interest is where this heads once the WC department is running.

📂Documents: records intake + round-tripPhase 1

Carrier files and medical records arrive, get auto-classified, summarized on your existing templates, and filed back into ProLaw. The summaries are live today. What is new is the automation around them: no more download, re-file, and re-name by hand.

Build first: it extends what is already proven and gives time back on day one.

⏱️Billing: billable time capturePhase 2

An agent drafts the 0.1s you forget from sent email and document time, builds the timesheet, and posts it to billing on your approval. Roughly 2 hours a week per attorney, recaptured. Heather on the 15th: "I might be able to survive more years."

Next: the firm-wide wedge Judy wants to take to Alex. Needs your billing-system connection.

✉️Emails: triage + filing to the claimPhase 3

Every incoming email, carrier, adjuster, medical, and court, read and filed to the right claim, with anything needing a decision flagged to the attorney. Solves Dina's pile of unsorted attachments.

Then: rounds out "all in one place" once documents and billing are flowing.
