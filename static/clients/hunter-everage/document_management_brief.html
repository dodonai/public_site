What stays yours

What this build does not do

👤

Nothing gets drafted

This pilot reads, routes, and files. It doesn't write a demand, a reply, or anything that goes to a client, opposing counsel, or a court. That's a separate, later conversation, not part of this build.

🔀

Exceptions flag, they don't guess

A fax the agent can't match to a matter, or a document that looks miscategorized, gets flagged for a person instead of filed on a guess.

Reliability, in plain terms

What "reliable" means on this build

Charles's concern is hallucinations in court-submitted work. Here's why that doesn't apply to this pilot.

✓ SOC 2 Certified ✓ HIPAA Compliant ✓ Tenant-resident ✓ BAA available
🔐

Routing and filing, not writing

Every action here is deterministic: match a document to a matter, save it to the systems you already use. There's no generation step where a hallucination could enter court-facing work, because nothing court-facing gets generated here.

🗂️

Filed where you already look

The agent connects to Egnyte the same way it connects to OneDrive and Smart Advocate: reads what it needs to route the document, writes it into the matter, and stops. Nothing lives anywhere else. (Exact Egnyte access mechanics: confirm with client before build.)
