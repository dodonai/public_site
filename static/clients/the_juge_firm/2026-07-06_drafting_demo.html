EXCEPTION AND ANSWER

Pelican Industrial Services, LLC and Great American Insurance Company, sought to be made defendants herein, through undersigned counsel, respond to the Petition of Darryl T. Boudreaux upon information and belief, aver as follows:

EXCEPTION

Plaintiff's claim for weekly compensation benefits has prescribed in whole or in part.

ANSWER TO PETITION

Defendants deny that plaintiff is entitled to any additional weekly benefits or medical care. On the contrary, plaintiff has received all benefits to which he is entitled under the Louisiana Workers' Compensation Law.

Defendants DENY plaintiff's allegations regarding his average weekly wage and weekly compensation rate.

Defendants ADMIT that plaintiff had an accident within the course and scope of employment.

Defendants DENY that plaintiff sustained an injury within the course and scope of employment with defendant.

Defendants ADMIT that plaintiff was an employee of defendant, Pelican Industrial Services, LLC, at the time of the alleged work-related accident.

Defendants deny that plaintiff is entitled to temporary total disability benefits under LSA-R.S. 23:1221(1), permanent and total disability under 23:1221(2), Supplemental Earnings Benefits under 23:1221(3), or any Schedule Benefits under 23:1221(4).

Defendants dispute that they unreasonably delayed or denied payment of any benefits and deny that they acted in an arbitrary and capricious manner in TERMINATING benefits; therefore, plaintiff is not entitled to penalties and attorney's fees.

WHEREFORE, defendants pray that this Answer be deemed good and sufficient, and that there be an order rendered in favor of defendants and against the plaintiff, dismissing plaintiff's Petition at plaintiff's costs.
