What changes for the floor

The point of the engagement isn't software. It's what a job looks like once the back office runs itself.

80% → 99.5%

Notice accuracy, the error source gone

Hand-keying a notice runs about 80% accurate, and a misspelled doctor or party name follows the job all the way to a transcript and summary you pay to redo. The OS reads and validates the notice, and a person confirms only the flagged fields, so accuracy lands near 99.5%.

Same day = paid

Invoice out the moment the transcript lands

Transcript and summary assemble onto a QuickBooks invoice automatically, queued for your biller. "The quicker we get them the invoice, the quicker we get paid" becomes the default, not the goal.

5-mi blast → best match

Reporter dispatch that actually chooses

Instead of an email blast to everyone within five miles, the OS matches the right reporter to the job and dispatches them with the notice and job sheet in hand.
