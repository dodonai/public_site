Card 1 · P1-01 · Acceptance clause 1

Portfolio Case Tracker

The litigation-event emails you already receive advance each matter's state on their own. Nobody updates the tracker. Read-only, no approval gate. Your working Kapitus spreadsheet is the starting model.

Trigger
A litigation-event email lands in eservice@. Supported events: complaint filed, answer served, discovery served, MSJ filed. Full vocabulary for Lauren to confirm.
Inputs
The event email + attachments · matter-match keys · the working Kapitus Matter Report spreadsheet (starting model + reconciliation baseline) · the Clio matter list (identity only — no Clio writes).
Decision rules
Classify the event, match the matter, map event → state transition. Only supported events advance state; the rest queue. Idempotent — a re-sent email never advances a matter twice. Starter state model: Filed → Served → Answered → Discovery → MSJ (Judgment/Default in Phase 2). Discovery responses received are an event but carry no LM&G deadline.
Outputs
Matter state updated on the firm-facing tracker, each change linked to its source email + timestamp. No writes to Clio or the document store.
Standards
Reconciles to the working spreadsheet (Lauren sign-off). Every state change traceable to a source. Replay-safe, no duplicates.
Exceptions
Case-number conflict (Germbusters) · no match, or matter in the spreadsheet but not Clio · unknown event type · discovery responses (recorded, explicitly no deadline).
Human-review gates
None on the tracker — read-only per the SOW. Exceptions route to Lauren's queue. Reconciliation is signed off by Lauren before acceptance.
Friction points
The full event vocabulary beyond the four named events must be confirmed · manually-set spreadsheet states with no event email are preserved and flagged, not overwritten · forwarded events with stripped headers reduce match confidence.

Card 2 · P1-02 · Acceptance clauses 2 & 3

Calendaring & Document Saving

Served documents arrive as email attachments; the agent files each to its matter and calendars the deadline through Clio's date rules, not a person at 6pm. Includes a daily digest of what got calendared the day before, and a switchable NetDocs ↔ OneDrive/SharePoint connector.

Trigger
A served document arrives as an email attachment in eservice@ — summons/complaint, proof of service, served discovery.
Inputs
Email + attachment(s) · matter keys + store location keys (Maison / Germbusters) · Clio date-calculation rules · the document-store connector (NetDocs today, SharePoint/OneDrive by configuration).
Decision rules
Classify the document, match the matter, file it with a durable receipt. Then the deadline decision: a served document with a response period → compute via Clio date rules, propose the entry, write behind a gate (Maison summons: 30 calendar days from personal service on Aug 25); served discovery responses → no new deadline. Ambiguous service facts → review, never guessed.
Outputs
(1) Document filed to the correct matter with a recoverable receipt · (2) a Clio calendar entry for the deadline (gated) · (3) the next-morning "what was calendared yesterday" digest with entry links and that day's exceptions.
Standards
Correct matter, folder/profile, correct date rule, deadline matches the attorney-expected date. Correct timezone/cutoff. Idempotent — a retry never double-files or double-calendars.
Exceptions
Name mismatch (Jamon vs James) · case-number conflict · ambiguous service date/method · no match or unrecognized doc · partial destination failure (filed but calendar failed, or vice versa) → flagged, not silent success.
Human-review gates
Clio calendar write is gated — Paul owns rule/exception decisions. Production document write is gated on Josh's approval of the switchable behavior; during acceptance, writes go only to the approved test location. The daily digest is Lauren's spot-check.
Friction points
NetDocs access still pending (vendor onboarding in progress) — production filing unproven until a scoped canary passes; the SharePoint canary already works, so the switchable design is de-risked · Clio date-rule coverage must be verified · which document types create deadlines needs Paul.

Card 3 · P1-03 · Acceptance clause 4

Cost Accounting in Clio

Reads the vendor's monthly invoice and per-filing receipts, splits every charge by case, and posts them into Clio through its API — so costs land on the client invoice next to your fees with no spreadsheet in between. About 3 paralegal hours a month, returned.

Trigger
A vendor invoice, monthly statement, or per-filing receipt arrives in invoice@.
Inputs
The invoice/statement PDF — sample: the 69-page Nationwide Legal statement (8-page account summary + underlying itemized invoices, $3,303.06 across many matters) · per-filing receipts · matter keys per line item · Clio matters & the cost/billing API.
Decision rules
Separate summary from detail so the account-summary pages are not double-counted. Extract per line: case/vendor reference, invoice number, service date, description, amount. Allocate each charge to a matter. Post to the right Clio matter with the correct billable/cost field, behind a gate. Deduplicate so re-processing never double-posts. Worked example, Maison #283: ENL-...1927 ($35) + ENL-...1939 ($95) + ENL-...1940 ($45) = $175.
Outputs
Per-matter cost entries in Clio (gated), landing on the client invoice next to fees · an allocation ledger (charge → matter → Clio record) · a monthly summary to Lauren.
Standards
Allocations reconcile to the statement total; no summary/detail double-count; correct matter, amount, billable/cost field; retry-safe.
Exceptions
Unmatched matter → queue, don't post · statement/detail overlap (a receipt also inside the statement) → dedupe or flag · credits/adjustments/amount mismatch → flag · ambiguous matter → queue.
Human-review gates
Clio cost posting is gated, separate from the calendar-write gate. Lauren approves the representative cost run before production posting. The monthly summary is the ongoing review surface.
Friction points
Clio billing/cost API capability is unproven and a called-out risk — we validate cost posting early, before committing dates · the Nationwide format is assumed representative (new formats = config inside the acceptance window) · the summary-vs-detail structure is the main parsing risk.
1

Vendor & billable treatment

Confirm the vendor set and that the Nationwide Legal statement is representative, and that charges post as client-billable costs (with any per-charge exceptions).

2

Summary & exceptions

Confirm the monthly summary recipient(s) and format, and that unmatched/credit lines hold in your queue for review (our default).

Card 4 · P1-04 · Acceptance clause 5

Deadline & Hearing Digest

One weekly email per attorney with every upcoming deadline and court hearing pulled from the Clio calendar. Nothing sneaks up on anyone.

Trigger
Schedule-driven (not email-driven): once per week at a confirmed day/time. Reads the Clio calendar — no mailbox involved.
Inputs
The Clio calendar (deadlines + hearings) · per-attorney calendar scope (mapping to confirm) · look-ahead window (to confirm) · recipients Josh, Lauren, Paul (confirmed) · the approved sender / delivery channel — an open decision (M365 Mail.Send from a dedicated mailbox vs an external service).
Decision rules
For each attorney, gather upcoming deadlines + hearings in the window from their scope; one email per attorney with only their items; empty-period handling ("nothing upcoming" vs suppress — confirm); duplicate-send protection (at most one send per intended weekly delivery).
Outputs
One weekly email per attorney listing their upcoming deadlines and court hearings.
Standards
Correct events per attorney, correct timezone · changed/cancelled events reflected · delivery confirmed; retry sends at most once per intended delivery.
Exceptions
Attorney with no upcoming items → per the confirmed empty-period rule · matter/event not mapped to an attorney → flag for routing, don't drop · send failure → retry once then alert, never double-send.
Human-review gates
Content, recipients & per-attorney routing confirmed before activation; each attorney verifies their own digest during acceptance. No standing gate once accepted — but the sender/channel decision is open and must be settled before go-live.
Friction points
The daily-vs-weekly distinction above · per-attorney calendar scope mapping needs Josh/Paul · the sender decision (M365 vs external) is unresolved and gates go-live · look-ahead window + weekly day/time need Paul/Josh.
