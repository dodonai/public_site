The walkthrough

Seven questions for each workflow

The point is to understand the work as it happens today. Rough answers are useful.

1

Trigger

What event, request, file, or matter state starts the work?

2

Inputs

Which files, matter fields, templates, and systems are used?

3

Steps and owners

Who does what today, and in what order?

4

Finished output

What work product or decision must result?

5

Human gates

Where must attorney or staff judgment remain in control?

6

Pain and baseline

How long does it take, how often does it occur, and where does it fail?

7

Win condition

Which measurable change would make the first build worthwhile?

How Wave 1 gets selected

Six filters, one first domino

1Return

Meaningful time, margin, capacity, quality, or cycle-time improvement.

2Speed to proof

A credible result within the first build phase.

3Frequency

Enough repetition for the value to compound.

4Source readiness

Inputs and approved examples exist and can be accessed.

5Integration load

The pilot can proceed without waiting on CARET or another vendor.

6Adoption

The team sees painful work removed and better work returned.
