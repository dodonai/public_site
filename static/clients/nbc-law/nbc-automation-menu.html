What we heard

Every deal is the same deal. The hours aren't coming down.

Skilled nursing and assisted living acquisitions, nationwide, each one a PSA plus an OTA, a lender checklist, and 40-plus closing documents. The documents are standard and the facts change, but almost every one is still built by opening the last deal's version and swapping the facts by hand. Two paralegals carry six timekeepers' closings.

You do this six times a week. It's the same thing over and over. This shouldn't take you three hours.
