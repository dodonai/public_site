The stuck point

Judge and district templates, solved first

Bryn, 5/18: "we have a folder for the judges … that's a big part of what the paralegal does." This is the litigation-specific piece, and it is the knowledge most at risk in a seat change.

⚖️Which template applies to this case?cluster C · weeks 1 to 3
Today
  • Complaint filed; judge unknown until the ECF assignment email arrives.
  • Paralegal reads the email, opens the judge folder, picks the scheduling-order and filing templates by memory.
  • Southern and Middle District differ; each judge may add their own rules on top of local rules.
→
With the OS
  • ECF assignment email is read; court, judge and magistrate are written to the Service.
  • The judge-folder library becomes data: every later draft (JSR, PSO, disclosures, filings) resolves its template set automatically, falling back to the local-rule template when the judge has none.
  • Sonya reviews the resolved set once per case instead of remembering it.

The method

SOP first, then build

Wave 1 built first and wrote the staff SOP afterwards. Litigation flips that. Each cluster gets a pre-build SOP written with Sonya and a litigation attorney, ratified by Bryn, then built to and accepted against. The same file becomes Sonya's training manual on day one.

1Seven pre-build SOPs
1Litigation intake and the deadline to file suit (L-01, L-02, L-03)
2Complaint and filing package (L-04, L-05, L-06)
3Judge and district template resolution + post-filing calendar (L-07, L-08, L-11)
4JSR / PSO and the scheduling conference (L-09, L-10)
5Mediation (L-12, L-13, L-14)
6Settlement and closeout (L-15, L-16, L-17, L-22)
7Friday report and litigation approval handoff (L-20, L-21)
2What each SOP pins down
→Trigger, required facts, and what stops the workflow when a fact is missing (it never guesses).
→Each step marked You do, The system does, or Stays your call, with the output and exactly where it lands.
→Data model: fields read, fields stamped, Egnyte folder and filename pattern, which calendars.
→The firm's decisions and inputs, with an owner, and a definition of done the canary is accepted against.
