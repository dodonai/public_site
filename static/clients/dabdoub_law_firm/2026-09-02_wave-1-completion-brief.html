The rule that never changes

📅

Every deadline

Anchor dates are entered by a person; the ladder and the calendars follow. Dates you already calendared are detected and respected.

✉️

Every send

Letters land as unsent drafts in the right Outlook or as Word files in AI output. A person reviews and sends.

📂

Every file move

Governed filing surfaces cards to named reviewers. Auto-verified means "nothing to check", not "filed". No approval, no move.
