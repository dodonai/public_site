Submission packet contents for your red-line

What the packet builder would bundle, in what order, for the two submission packets. The appeal packet is already confirmed (your 6/18 answers). The initial-claim packet is a placeholder that needs your edits.

How it works either way: the builder reads the client folder, picks the documents for each section below, and produces a plan (ordered list plus an index) that an assistant or attorney signs off before anything is assembled. Anything marked DO NOT USE in the folder is held out and cannot be included. Nothing is sent by the system.

Initial-claim submission packet placeholder, needs red-line

Fires when an initial claim is ready to file.

  1. Cover letterDrafted separately: the Notice of Representation letter to the carrier, adapted as the filing cover. Attorney signs.
  2. Claim formsThe carrier's completed claim packet as returned by the client and providers (claimant statement, attending physician statement, employer statement, any carrier-specific forms).
  3. Medical recordsTreating records and provider letters, plus any objective testing on file:
    • Treating and provider records
    • Provider support letters
    • Functional capacity evaluation (FCE)
    • Cardiopulmonary exercise test (CPET)
    • Neuropsychological evaluation
Held out of every packet: anything marked DO NOT USE, surveillance, correspondence, firm work product, portal logins and passwords, and duplicates flagged for review.

Appeal submission packet confirmed 6/18

For reference, so the two read side by side. Fires when the appeal is ready to file.
