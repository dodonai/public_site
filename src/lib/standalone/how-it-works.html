Stage 01
Organize

You explain the job. We reconstruct the rest.

Most firms do not have a process manual for the work that matters. That is fine. The people doing the work know it, and the systems already contain the evidence.

01

Talk us through one real task.

Where it starts, what a good result looks like, and where judgment belongs. No questionnaire masquerading as simplicity.

02

We go find the work.

Our AI-assisted discovery process finds the forms, exemplars, records, recurring patterns and unwritten steps already buried in your systems.

03

You confirm what we found.

Mostly yes, no, or “that one should have died in 2019.” You correct our draft. You do not write the manual.

04

We turn it into a buildable workflow.

Inputs, steps, outputs, human decisions and approved examples are documented and priced before anything gets built.

A real example
“We always start from the last good demand.”
What we found: 214 demands, nine templates and two that carried nearly all the recent volume. Instead of asking the team to sort through them, we narrowed the field and brought back the likely exemplars for confirmation.
The result: a standard template for automation, a workflow the team recognizes, a clear line around what stays human, and a fixed price before development starts.
