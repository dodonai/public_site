Step 02
Organize

Turn the pile into raw material.

Documents and data classified. Systems and workflows mapped. All of it searchable. This is the foundation every agent builds on.

02.1 · DOCUMENTS & DATA

Storage, standardized.

One structure for every matter, created automatically the moment a case opens. Existing files migrate into it; new files are born organized. No more "New folder (3)".

Not keyword search: answers. Documents, mail, and billing in one semantic index, so the firm's knowledge responds in sentences with sources attached.
