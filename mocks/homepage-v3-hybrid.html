Human in the loop

The work is automated. The judgment never is.

The overnight run produces drafts, flags, and signals. Not one of them is a decision. Everything waits at your desk until someone at the firm approves it. That is not a limitation we engineered around. It is the architecture.

How we keep you in control

A person releases everything

Drafts queue in your folders and your drafts box. Nothing reaches a client, a court, or a counterparty until someone inside the firm sends it.

Never trained on your data
